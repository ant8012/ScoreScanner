chord_rec.onnx, chord_rec_dict.txt

The on-device chord reader is PaddleOCR's PP-OCRv6 small text recognition model, fine-tuned on chord symbols
(tools/chordfind in the ScoreScanner repository) and exported to ONNX.

PaddleOCR and its PP-OCRv6 models: Apache License 2.0, copyright PaddlePaddle Authors,
https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR. The licence text is the Apache License 2.0 (as in
App/SampleAssets/Licenses/copisteria-Apache-2.0.txt). This file is a modified version of their model: its weights
were trained further on synthetic chord-symbol images rendered with MuseScore.
